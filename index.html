01

About

My work connects mechanical design, experimental validation, hydrodynamic measurement, and reproducible data analysis across the full research cycle—from apparatus design to performance verification and technical documentation.

  • Biomimetic marine propulsion

    Fish-like tail-beat propulsion, multi-DOF motion, recoil dynamics, thrust generation, and propulsive performance.

  • Fully passive hydrokinetic systems

    Flapping-foil turbines, self-starting mechanisms, bidirectional operation, crank-rocker transmission, and energy harvesting.

  • Experimental hydrodynamics

    Water tunnels, recirculating channels, force and torque measurement, PIV, LDV, ADV, signal processing, and uncertainty assessment.

  • Test and evaluation engineering

    Measurement procedures, experimental environment development, repeatability, data traceability, and draft technical standards.
