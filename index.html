한국어

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology. I work on experimental hydrodynamics for marine propulsion and hydrokinetic energy. I design test apparatus and measurement systems, set up the experiments — at scales from laboratory tanks to large-scale test facilities — and carry them through to data analysis.

My current work covers fully passive flapping-foil turbines, underwater vehicles using biomimetic caudal-fin propulsion, and test and evaluation procedures for marine unmanned systems.
