한국어

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology, studying underwater vehicle propulsion and hydrokinetic energy experimentally. From laboratory tanks to large-scale test facilities, I design the test apparatus and instrumentation, set up and run the measurements, and see the work through to the analysis.

I measure the motion and loading of devices that move in water, and establish why they behave as they do. Current subjects are fully passive flapping-foil turbines and underwater vehicles driven by biomimetic caudal-fin propulsion. I am also establishing test and evaluation procedures for marine unmanned systems, so that the same device returns the same number no matter who performs the measurement.
