Dong-Geon Kim

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology · 김동건

I build and instrument the physical test rigs that decide whether a marine propulsion or energy-harvesting concept actually works in water. Most of that happens in a circulating water channel: a mechanism arrives — a pair of foils that should flap on their own, a tail that should make thrust the way a fish's does — and my job is to turn it into something measurable.

I work on fully passive flapping-foil turbines, biomimetic caudal-fin propulsion, and the measurement procedures that let two laboratories report the same number for the same thruster.
