Dong-Geon
Kim, Ph.D.

Experimental research scientist specializing in biomimetic marine propulsion and hydrokinetic turbine systems.

I develop passive and energy-efficient underwater mechanisms inspired by aquatic locomotion, with an emphasis on water-tunnel experiments, hydrodynamic performance evaluation, fluid-structure interaction, and mechanical design.

About

My research combines experimental mechanics, hydrodynamics, and mechanism design to investigate propulsion and energy conversion in biologically inspired marine systems.

  • Biomimetic propulsion, energy harvesting, and aquatic locomotion

  • Experimental hydrodynamics and fluid-structure interaction

    Large-scale water-tunnel and recirculating-flume facilities.

  • Mechanical and mechanism design for underwater systems
